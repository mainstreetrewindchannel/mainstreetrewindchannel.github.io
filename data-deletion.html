User Data Deletion

Effective date: October 21, 2026

channel-studio is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Main Street Rewind channel to post content to its own Facebook Page, Instagram account and YouTube channel. It does not collect or store personal data about viewers or any other users.

What the app stores

Only the access credentials the channel owner granted to publish to the channel's own accounts. These are stored encrypted and are never shared.

How to delete your data

  1. Facebook and Instagram: go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find channel-studio, and click Remove. This revokes all access immediately.
  2. Google and YouTube: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, select channel-studio, and remove access.
  3. Request deletion of stored credentials: email mainstreetrewindchannel@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Any stored credentials are deleted within 7 days and you will receive a confirmation.
