Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

What this app is

channel-studio is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the Main Street Rewind YouTube channel. It is used only to manage that one channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to it.

YouTube API Services

channel-studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data handled through these services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

  • Videos, captions, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and playlists on the Main Street Rewind channel, in order to upload and update them.
  • Aggregated YouTube Analytics reports for the channel (views, watch time, audience retention, and revenue once available), in order to measure performance.

The app does not access, collect or store personal information about viewers or any other YouTube users.

How information is stored and shared

Access credentials are stored in an encrypted secret store and are used only by the channel owner's automated publishing jobs. Analytics reports are stored privately for the channel owner's use. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access

Access granted to channel-studio can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Stored credentials are deleted when access is revoked or the tool is retired.
